This policy explains what information we collect when you use nycatvwarehouse.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and your choices.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us**
- Order details: your name, email address, phone number, delivery or billing address, the items you order, your pickup location, and any notes you add.
- Messages you send us through the contact form, email, phone, text message or Facebook Messenger.

**Payment information.** Card and other payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe's secure payment page. If you choose financing, Affirm handles your application and payments. We never see or store your full card number. We receive limited details from Stripe, such as the amount paid, the payment status, and your name, email and address.

**Information collected automatically**
- Device and usage information such as your browser type, pages viewed, and the approximate area you visit from, collected through cookies and similar tools from Google (Google Ads / Google tag).
- Your shopping cart is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there when you come back. It stays on your device.

## 2. How we use your information
- To process and fulfill your order: take payment, arrange pickup, local delivery or shipping, and contact you about it.
- To answer questions, provide service and parts support, and handle returns, refunds, warranty requests and claims.
- To prevent fraud and keep our Site and payments secure.
- To send you marketing emails and offers (see Marketing below).
- To measure how the Site is used and to show our ads to people who visited the Site.
- To follow the law, including tax and record-keeping rules.

We do not sell your personal information.

**Marketing.** We may use your name, email address and phone number to send you news, offers, promotions and updates about our products and services. Every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe. We send marketing text messages only if you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out at any time (see Section 6). Opting out of marketing does not stop messages about your order, delivery, pickup, returns or warranty.

## 3. Who we share it with
We share information only with companies that help us run the store, and only as needed:
- **Stripe** (payment processing) and **Affirm** (financing, if you choose it).
- **Delivery and freight carriers**, to ship or deliver your order. They receive your name, phone number and address.
- **FormSubmit**, which delivers messages and order requests from our forms to our email.
- **Google**, for advertising and site analytics.
- **Facebook / Meta**, if you contact us through Messenger.
- **Professional advisers** (accountants, lawyers, insurers) and **government authorities** when the law requires it.

If we ever sell or transfer the business, customer information may be transferred as part of that sale.

## 4. Cookies and advertising
We use cookies and similar technology from Google to understand how visitors use the Site and to measure our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's tools at adssettings.google.com. Blocking cookies will not stop your cart from working.

## 5. How long we keep information
We keep order and payment records for as long as needed to complete your order, handle returns and warranty questions, and meet tax and legal requirements, generally 7 years. Messages are kept as long as needed to help you.

## 6. Your choices and rights
You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, unless we must keep it for legal or accounting reasons. Depending on where you live (including New Jersey and New York), you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to know, correct, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or targeted advertising. To opt out of marketing, click the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, reply STOP to any marketing text, or call or email us. To make any other request, call 646-943-1858 or email sales@nycatvwarehouse.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

## 7. Security
Payments are handled by Stripe over an encrypted connection. We limit who can see order information. No website or system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children
The Site is for adults. You must be 18 or older to place an order. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child gave us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Other sites
The Site links to other sites (such as Stripe, Affirm, Facebook and Google). Their privacy practices are their own.
