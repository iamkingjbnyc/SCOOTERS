Policies

Return policy

Last updated October 25, 2026. By placing an order you agree to this policy. In short: 14-day returns on new, unused units, a 20% restocking fee, and the customer pays for return freight.

1. Return window

You may request a return within 14 days of delivery (shipped and delivered orders) or of the day you pick up your unit.

2. Condition of the unit

The unit must be new and unused: never started, ridden or modified, with every part, the original crate or packaging, and all documents. Units that are used, damaged after delivery, altered or missing parts may be refused or have the refund reduced.

3. How to start a return

Call 646-943-1858 or email sales@nycatvwarehouse.com with your order details. You need our approval before sending anything back. Returns sent without approval will not be accepted.

4. Restocking fee and return shipping

  • A restocking fee of 20% of the unit price applies to returns.
  • The customer pays for return freight. You are responsible for the cost of shipping the unit back to us, and for arranging it once we approve the return.
  • Original shipping and delivery charges are not refunded.

5. Deposit

The $100 deposit on pickup and local delivery orders is non-refundable. It covers assembly and/or delivery charges. The only exception is item 8 below (if we cannot fill your order).

6. Shipped orders: inspect on delivery

  • Inspect the crate and the unit when it arrives, with the driver present if possible.
  • Write any visible damage or missing boxes on the delivery receipt before you sign.
  • Report damage or missing parts to us within 72 hours of delivery, with photos. Keep all packaging.
  • Damage that is not reported in time may not be eligible for a claim.

7. Pickup and local delivery

Inspect your unit before you accept it or take it away. Once you have accepted it, the rules above apply to any return.

8. Orders we cannot fill

If a unit is sold out or we cannot fill your order, we will cancel it and refund everything you paid, including the deposit.

9. Refused deliveries

If you refuse a delivery or miss your delivery appointment without telling us, you are responsible for the freight in both directions and any applicable fees, which will be deducted from your refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within about 7-10 business days after we receive and inspect the unit, less the fees above. Your bank may take additional time to post it.
