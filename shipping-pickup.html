How it works

Shipping, delivery & pickup

Order online, then choose the way you want to get it at checkout. Every in-stock model can be shipped, delivered locally for free, or picked up.

Free pickup

Pick up your order at either location: 207 Gates Rd, Little Ferry, NJ 07643 (Mon–Fri 10am–5pm, Sat–Sun closed) or 4301 Bronx Blvd, Bronx, NY (Mon–Fri 10am–6pm). Choose your location at checkout. We’ll contact you when it’s ready. Pay a $100 non-refundable deposit online; the balance is due at pickup.

Free

Free local delivery

Delivery is free across all of NYC and a 20-mile radius of Little Ferry, NJ. Anywhere on Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk) and up to 80 miles from us is $100. Enter your ZIP at checkout to see your price. Pay a $100 non-refundable deposit online; the balance is due on delivery.

Free

Shipped to you

Ordering from out of town? We ship to your address anywhere in the continental U.S. (the 48 contiguous states and Washington, DC; not Alaska, Hawaii or U.S. territories). The rate depends on the type of vehicle and is shown before you pay. Shipped orders are paid in full online.

Rate at checkout

Shipping rates

  • E-Bikes: $125 per unit
  • Scooters: $249 per unit
  • Dirt Bikes: $199 per unit
  • ATVs: $299 per unit
  • Go-Karts: $299 per unit
  • UTVs: $499 per unit
  • Helmets: $25 per unit

What to expect when your shipped unit arrives

Your unit arrives about 90% assembled, securely packed in a crate. Handlebars, wheels and a few other parts may need to be attached before it is ready to ride. Assembly instructions are included.

How payment works

  • Free pickup and free local delivery: pay a $100 deposit online. The deposit is non-refundable. It covers assembly and/or delivery charges and is credited toward your order total. You pay the remaining balance when you pick up or when we deliver.
  • Shipping: pay the full amount online, including shipping.

After you order

We’ll contact you within 1 business day to confirm your order and arrange pickup, delivery or shipping.

Before you buy

Please check your state and local rules for registration, titling and where you may ride each type of vehicle. Not sure? Ask us or call 646-943-1858.
